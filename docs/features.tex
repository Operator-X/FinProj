\section{Phase 1: Data and unit economics}

\section{Phase 2: Forecasting}
\begin{featuretable}
LightGBM forecast (Tweedie loss) & Predicts next week's sales; handles the 52\% of weeks with zero sales & \code{engine/forecast.py} & Product $\to$ Forecast \\
Seasonal naive and SARIMAX & Simple benchmarks to beat & \code{engine/forecast.py} & Evidence $\to$ forecast pop-up \\
Walk-forward testing & Tests forecasts without ever peeking at the future & \code{engine/forecast.py} & Evidence \\
Leakage fix (last week's price) & Stops the model using information it would not have had & \code{engine/forecast.py} & Covered by tests \\
Money-based scoring & Turns forecast error into stockout losses and cash tied up & \code{engine/forecast.py} & Evidence \\
\end{featuretable}

\section{Phase 3: Causal price sensitivity}
\begin{featuretable}
Double ML & Measures how price really changes demand, removing coincidences such as Black Friday & \code{engine/elasticity.py} & Evidence page (main chart) \\
Cross-fitting in time blocks & Keeps the ML models from leaking future data into the estimate & \code{engine/elasticity.py} & Evidence $\to$ statistics \\
Product fixed effects, clustered standard errors & Compares each product with itself; honest error bars & \code{engine/elasticity.py} & Evidence (error bars) \\
Naive and pooled regression & Shows what goes wrong without causal methods (about 3$\times$ overstated) & \code{engine/elasticity.py} & Evidence headline ``3$\times$'' \\
DerSimonian--Laird shrinkage & Stabilises noisy per-product estimates by pulling them toward the category average & \code{engine/elasticity.py} & Product pop-up \\
Corrections (log fix, sign restriction, cap at $-5$) & Keeps estimates economically sensible & \code{engine/elasticity.py} & Product pop-up chips \\
Robustness check (weeks with sales) & Confirms the result holds under a second definition & \code{engine/elasticity.py} & Evidence $\to$ statistics \\
\end{featuretable}

\section{Phase 4: Price optimiser}
\begin{featuretable}
Profit-maximising price search & Finds the best price (401-point grid, then Brent refinement) & \code{engine/optimize.py} & Overview cards, product pop-up \\
Price floor (1.15$\times$ cost; 1.00$\times$ if stock is old) & Never sells below cost; allows clearance of stock older than 8 weeks of supply & \code{engine/optimize.py} & Product pop-up chips \\
$\pm$15\% price band & Never shocks buyers with large jumps & \code{engine/optimize.py} & Overview card text \\
Margin vs cash-recovery modes & Optimise this week's profit, or clear stock to free cash & \code{engine/optimize.py} & Scenario lab goal switch \\
Uncertainty band & Re-solves the price under 200 draws of price sensitivity & \code{engine/optimize.py} & Product $\to$ Simulate \\
Lerner discount test & Checks whether any discount could add profit (none of 72 do) & \code{engine/finance.py} & Evidence, Overview \\
\end{featuretable}

\section{Phase 5A: AI season planner (reinforcement learning)}
\begin{featuretable}
26-week season simulator & Sells one lot of stock; leftovers go to an outlet at half of cost & \code{engine/rl.py} & Season planner \\
Bayesian demand learning & The planner updates its estimate of price sensitivity from its own sales & \code{engine/rl.py} & Season replay \\
PPO agent & The AI that decides each week whether to hold the price or start clearing & \code{engine/rl.py} & Season replay (``AI season planner'') \\
Exact plan and oracle (dynamic programming) & Benchmarks: the best possible plan, and one that knows true demand & \code{engine/rl.py} & Season replay $\to$ More options \\
Value of information & What knowing true demand would be worth & \code{engine/rl.py} & ``Value of knowing the truth'' note \\
Out-of-sample scorecard & Results over 30 unseen seasons per product & \code{engine/rl.py} & Bottom chart of the Season planner \\
\end{featuretable}
